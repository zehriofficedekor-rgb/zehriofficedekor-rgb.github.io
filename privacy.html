Privacy Policy

ZehnurFavors Social Publisher · Last updated: October 28, 2026

ZehnurFavors Social Publisher ("the App") is an internal tool operated by ZehnurFavors, a small handmade gift shop based in Istanbul, Turkey, that sells on Etsy (etsy.com/shop/ZehnurFavors). The App is used only by the shop owners to publish the shop's own product content to the shop's own social media accounts.

1. Who uses the App

The App is not offered to the public. It has no sign-up and no third-party users. The only account connected to it is the ZehnurFavors business account on each platform (Pinterest and Instagram).

2. What data the App accesses

  • Pinterest: the list of boards on the ZehnurFavors account (to choose where to save a Pin), the ability to create Pins on those boards, and basic Pin analytics for our own Pins.
  • Instagram: the ability to publish posts and Reels to the ZehnurFavors business account, and basic insights for our own posts.
  • Our own product data: titles, photos and links of our own Etsy listings.

The App does not access, collect or store personal data of any other Pinterest or Instagram user, including followers, commenters or visitors.

3. How data is used

Data is used only to create and schedule posts and Pins promoting our own products, and to see how our own content performs. It is never sold, rented, shared with advertisers, or used to build profiles of people.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in our private automation environment and are never published or shared. We keep only the minimum data needed to run the App. Access can be revoked at any time from the platform's account settings, which immediately stops the App from working.

5. Third-party services

To create post text and images, the App may send our own product titles and product photos to AI services (such as Anthropic and Replicate). No personal data of other users is sent to these services.

6. Deletion

We can delete any data the App holds on request. Disconnecting the App from the Pinterest or Instagram account settings also ends all access.
